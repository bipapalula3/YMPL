Kang Sung Hoon is a singer who has been loved for a long time for his deep emotions and charming voice. His music conveys warmth and sincerity that caresses the hearts of listeners. He is an artist who stays by his fans' side and delivers emotion with his unchanging appearance.

Birth of a Group and the Beginning of Solo Activities
Kang Sung Hoon debuted as a member of an idol group and enjoyed explosive popularity. Since then, he has built his own musical world as a solo artist and continued his activities steadily. His musical journey encompasses various genres and has secured a wide fan base.
 
✧

The Depth of Emotional Ballads
Kang Sung Hoon's music mainly consists of ballads that stand out for their delicate expression of emotions. His songs bring comfort and emotion to listeners and are remembered for a long time.
