Dominant MBTI

The overall trend of this week's new releases reflects the characteristics of the 'ES (Extroverted-Sensing)' type, showing a strong inclination to pursue enjoyment through active and realistic approaches. The abundance of positive and energetic songs makes them suitable for energizing daily life, while tracks that touch upon deep emotions encourage introspection on relationships. The coexistence of various genres like hip-hop, dance, and ballads that satisfy a wide range of tastes also resembles the inclusive nature of ES.

This week's new releases are expected to bring particular satisfaction to listeners with an 'ES' inclination. Recommended for those who wish to gain active and positive energy or those who want to resonate with realistic emotions. With a diverse lineup of songs in various moods, it will offer an enjoyable listening experience for anyone in need of a mood change or seeking new musical experiences.
