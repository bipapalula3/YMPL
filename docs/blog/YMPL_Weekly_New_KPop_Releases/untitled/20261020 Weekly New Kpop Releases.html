대표 MBTI 분석

Dominant MBTI

The most prominent MBTI tendencies permeating this week's new songs are ESTP and INFP. NMIXX's 'Birthday Wish' effectively conveys the free-spirited and festive atmosphere characteristic of ESTP, while Ourealgoat's 'Still in My Heart' delivers the delicate and emotional resonance of INFP. ESTP embeds a tendency to focus on tangible enjoyment and the present, whereas INFP captures the pursuit of deep emotional connection and ideals within their music.
