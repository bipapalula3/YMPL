MBTI Trend

This week's new releases exhibit a flow centered around two axes: emotional exploration and self-expression. Tracks like eaJ's 'anxiety', HAEUN's 'Still the Sun, Still the Moon', Kim Feel's 'Circle', Yoon Hyun Sang's 'Subtle', 제현 (OMEGA X)'s 'Love and Hate', Verbal Jint, NOCHANG, B-Free, YULEUM's 'What If', BOL4's 'Golden Hour', Crush & IU's 'seesaw', ONEW's 'Illusion', WOODZ's 'Come into my world!', and jeebanoff's 'Lie' delve into inner emotions with subtlety, revealing emotional tendencies akin to INFP and ISFP. Conversely, songs such as ILLIT's 'You’re Under Arrest', Dakshood & Yanghongwon's 'Plan B', BABYMONSTER's 'DESERVE', LISA's 'Tarantino', KACHISAN's 'The Last Monster', Babo's 'Wah', KANGDANIEL's 'Blue', YUHZ's 'B.B.B', B THE HOOD's 'CHOP!', wonjin's 'Yuranga', IVE's 'Looks Can Kill', and KDA, League of Legends, i-dle, Mimi Webb, Sophie Powers, Miyeon, Soyeon's 'FIRESTARTER' showcase extroverted and active tendencies, similar to ESTP and ESFP, with strong beats and confident messages. Meanwhile, OH MY GIRL's 'FLY HIGH Instrumental', The Stray's 'voyage', EL CAPITXN & BEAUTY SCHOOL DROPOUT's 'Don't Say Love', It's's 'YOUNGONE (01)', LNGSHOT's 'Masterpiece', Lee Eun Sang's 'Garden of Dreams', 야자수(Yajasu)'s 'Island', and Damons Year's 'EGERO' enrich the overall atmosphere, forming connections with various MBTI types.

대표 MBTI 분석

이번 주 K-POP 신보들은 내면의 깊은 감정을 탐구하는 'INFP' 성향과 강렬한 에너지를 발산하며 자신감을 드러내는 'ESTP' 성향이 두드러집니다. INFP 성향의 곡들은 섬세한 가사와 멜로디로 청자들의 공감을 이끌어내며, ESTP 성향의 곡들은 파워풀한 퍼포먼스와 중독성 있는 비트로 듣는 이들을 사로잡습니다. 이러한 두 가지 상반된 매력이 공존하며 이번 주 음악 트렌드를 이끌고 있습니다.

Dominant MBTI

This week's K-POP new releases highlight the 'INFP' tendency, which explores deep inner emotions, and the 'ESTP' tendency, which radiates strong energy and expresses confidence. INFP-leaning songs evoke listener empathy with delicate lyrics and melodies, while ESTP-leaning songs captivate listeners with powerful performances and addictive beats. These two contrasting appeals coexist and lead this week's music trend.

✧

총평

감성적인 깊이와 폭발적인 에너지를 동시에 즐기고 싶은 분들에게 이번 주 신보들을 추천합니다. MBTI로 본다면, 내면의 목소리에 귀 기울이는 INFP 성향이나, 세상에 자신의 존재감을 확실히 드러내고 싶은 ESTP 성향을 가진 분들에게 특히 매력적인 라인업이 될 것입니다. 잔잔한 감동부터 짜릿한 쾌감까지, 다채로운 음악 경험을 통해 한 주간의 활력을 얻어가시길 바랍니다.

Conclusion

We recommend this week's new releases to those who wish to enjoy both emotional depth and explosive energy simultaneously. From an MBTI perspective, this lineup will be particularly appealing to individuals with an INFP tendency, who listen to their inner voice, or those with an ESTP tendency, who want to clearly express their presence to the world. We hope you gain a week's worth of vitality through a diverse musical experience, from serene emotions to thrilling exhilaration.
