Music & Album Style

The music of this month's rookies stands out for its diverse sounds and attempts to cross genres. They are building their own unique musical worlds by combining various elements such as hip-hop, R&B, and EDM based on trendy pop sounds. Delicate emotional expressions and honest lyrics evoke empathy from listeners, enhancing the overall quality of the album. Lucid Beats' experimental sounds offer freshness to listeners.

성장 가능성과 기대

Future Potential

The rookie artists debuting this month show excellent musical capabilities and unlimited potential, making us look forward to their future growth. If they continue to expand their musical spectrum without losing their own colors, they have the potential to grow into the next generation of leaders in the K-POP scene. We look forward to seeing them show a more developed image through constant effort and experimental attempts.
