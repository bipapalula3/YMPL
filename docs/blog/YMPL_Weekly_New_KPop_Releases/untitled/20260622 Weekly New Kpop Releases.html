Conclusion

With a prevalence of emotionally rich songs centered around MBTI's 'N' and 'F' aspects, this week's K-pop releases will offer significant comfort and resonance, especially to **INFP, INFJ, ENFP, and ENFJ** types who seek self-exploration and emotional sharing. Furthermore, the positive and energetic tracks will provide a good boost of vitality to extroverted and enjoyment-seeking types like **ESFP and ENTP**.
